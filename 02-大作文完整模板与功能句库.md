### 单幅图画：共同活动与注意力

- **材料：** 单幅图画
- **适用：** 适合图中人物共同参与一项活动，却因其他行为而疏于交流的场景。2015年聚会图可完整填充；不适合把所有单幅图都解释成“科技有害”。
- **路线：** 场景与动作 → 注意力问题 → 交流机制 → 具体行动

The picture shows [a group of people] [taking part in a shared activity], each [doing something that draws attention away from the others]. Although they are together, their attention seems to be directed elsewhere. The scene raises a question about what it means to be genuinely present when we spend time with other people.

Meaningful interaction requires more than being in the same place. Listening, responding and noticing another person’s expression allow us to understand what that person is trying to share. When these small acts of attention are repeatedly interrupted, a conversation can lose its continuity, and someone who wants to speak may feel overlooked. The problem is not the existence of another useful activity or device, but the way it takes the place of the interaction for which people have gathered. A shared occasion becomes valuable when those involved have a real opportunity to contribute.

We can therefore make a deliberate effort to protect time for conversation. During [a suitable occasion], we might [a practical action that reduces distraction], while allowing for genuine emergencies. Giving others our attention is a modest act, but it helps turn physical company into meaningful companionship.

替换说明：people槽填人物名词短语；taking part槽和doing槽均填-ing短语，如 *four friends / having dinner / checking a mobile phone*。末段action槽填动词原形，如 *put our phones aside*。注意：each looking中的each说明各人分别做同一动作，不需要再接are。完整填充见2015年范文。

### 行为对照：要求别人，还是以身作则

- **材料：** 行为对照图画
- **适用：** 适合两幅图或同一幅图中的左右场景形成行为对照。2016年材料的左右场景可以这样写，不能仅凭人物位置推断善恶。
- **路线：** 左右行为 → 言行差异 → 示范的作用 → 引导与支持

The scenes compare two ways of [guiding someone toward a goal]. In one, [a person] [gives an instruction but behaves inconsistently with it]; in the other, [the same person] [puts the advice into practice]. The contrast highlights a difference between telling others what to do and showing that the same expectation matters in one’s own life.

Advice gains credibility when words and conduct support each other. A person asked to change may reasonably wonder why an instruction should be taken seriously if the speaker routinely ignores it. A visible example offers something more concrete: it shows how a valued activity can fit into everyday life and what commitment to it actually involves. For a parent or teacher, this does not require a perfect performance. It requires a willingness to make a consistent effort, acknowledge difficulties and offer the support that others need to begin.

Those who give guidance should therefore examine their own conduct as well as the demands they make. By [a specific act of demonstration] and [a related form of support], they can make their expectations easier to understand. An example is most useful when it encourages others to act thoughtfully, rather than simply requiring them to copy every detail.

替换说明：ways of后接-ing；人物后的两个大槽均须填完整谓语，如 *watches television while telling his son to study / reads beside the child*，不能填孤立的watching。By后的两项保持-ing平行，如 *making time for reading and discussing difficult passages together*。完整应用见2016年范文。

### 时间变化：角色改变与关系延续

- **材料：** 时间变化图画
- **适用：** 适合两幅画呈现同一人物或关系在不同时间的变化。2014年母女题的重点是照护角色变化及关怀延续，不必写成价值观相反的两个场景。
- **路线：** 过去与现在 → 角色变化 → 具体照护与尊重 → 持续关怀

The two pictures show [the same people or relationship] at different stages of life. In the earlier scene, [a complete description of the earlier action]. Years later, [a complete description of the later action]. What changes is [the role or practical arrangement], while [the continuing bond or responsibility] remains. The pictures invite us to consider how care can respond to changing needs.

Care has value because it is directed toward a particular person. The assistance that is useful at one stage of life may be unnecessary or inadequate at another. Spending time together allows family members to notice such changes, listen to one another’s preferences and decide what help would actually make daily life easier. This practical attention also protects dignity: needing support does not mean losing the right to express a preference or take part in a decision. A lasting relationship therefore calls for both willingness to help and sensitivity to how that help is offered.

We should turn concern into regular actions, such as [two relevant forms of practical care]. Responsibilities can be shared according to people’s circumstances, with further assistance sought when necessary. The aim is to keep affection responsive and respectful, so that a changing role need not mean a weakening bond.

替换说明：两处description均填完整句；What changes is后的槽用名词短语，单数主语时后面的谓语用remains。such as后填名词或-ing短语，如 *keeping in touch and making time for visits*。完整应用见2014年范文；文化传承中的代际变化需重写主体机制，不能保留家庭照护细节。

### 动态图表：增长、需求与实际价值

- **材料：** 动态统计图表
- **适用：** 适合跨年份统计，先概括总体走势，再选择代表数据比较。2025年家电题是每百户平均台数，不能把“超过100台”写成超过100%的家庭拥有。
- **路线：** 总体变化与代表数据 → 可能条件与实际需求 → 使用价值 → 质量与维护

The [table or chart] compares [the measured indicator] for [the categories] between [starting year] and [ending year], with figures expressed in [unit]. An increase is recorded in each category, although the scale of the change differs. The figure for [category A] rises from [initial value] to [final value], while [category B] and [category C] show smaller gains. The main feature is a general expansion accompanied by a particularly marked rise in the figure for [category A].

Several factors may help explain this pattern. Greater affordability, easier access or changing practical needs may encourage more households to acquire useful products, though the relative importance of these factors depends on the circumstances. More importantly, an increase in ownership can create opportunities to save time or meet previously unmet needs. Those opportunities become lasting benefits only when the products work reliably and households can afford their use and maintenance. The quality of the service they provide matters alongside the quantity purchased.

The next step should therefore be to make growth useful and sustainable. Manufacturers can improve reliability, energy efficiency and repair access, while consumers can consider actual needs and running costs before buying. In this way, increased ownership can serve everyday life without making accumulation an end in itself.

替换说明：本模板的主体限定为产品或设备拥有量，不直接套到人口、比例或公共设施数量上。indicator槽可填 *the number of household appliances per 100 households*；数值须与单位配套。The figure为单数主语，故接rises；若直接以Air conditioners为主语则接rise。三个系列并非都上升时，必须改掉each category和general expansion。完整填充见2025年范文，其中对统计边界的说明保留现稿。

### 静态图表：态度分布与使用条件

- **材料：** 静态调查图表
- **适用：** 适合同一调查时点的类别分布，或两个分别报告不同指标的图表。2026年接受程度与主要顾虑是两组统计，不能拼接成同一群体的行为原因。
- **路线：** 分别描述分布 → 兴趣与顾虑 → 可靠性和成本 → 具体改进

The charts report a survey of [attitudes toward a technology or service] and [the concerns associated with its use]. In the first chart, [category A] accounts for [figure], compared with [figure] for [category B] and [figure] for [category C]. In the second, [the leading concern] ranks first, followed by [another concern]. Taken together, the findings present interest in a possible benefit alongside reservations about its practical delivery.

This combination deserves a considered response. People may welcome a service that helps them [a concrete task], yet remain uncertain about whether it will work when needed. Reliability matters because an unpredictable product can create extra work instead of reducing it. Affordability matters too: a useful service cannot benefit people who are unable to meet its continuing costs. Rather than dismissing these concerns as resistance to change, providers can treat them as information about what users need in order to make a confident decision.

Improvement should therefore begin with [the most relevant practical priority]. Providers can explain limitations clearly, offer accessible assistance and show how common problems will be handled. Users can then judge whether the service suits their circumstances. The goal is dependable help, with adoption guided by usefulness rather than novelty alone.

替换说明：accounts for后接占比；rank first只说明当前排序，不表示增长最快。help them后接动词原形；begin with后接名词或-ing。若一图统计人数、一图统计百分比，应分别说明，不能直接比较数值大小。完整应用见2026年范文。

### 图画与图表结合：设施数量与使用体验

- **材料：** 图画与图表组合
- **适用：** 适合图画呈现生活场景、图表提供相关数量变化的材料组合。2024年公园题可用：两种材料均需描述，再从共同议题展开。
- **路线：** 分别描述图画与图表 → 共同议题 → 可达性与使用条件 → 配套措施

The picture shows [people] [using a public facility], with [a person] commenting on [an improvement visible in the scene]. The accompanying chart records a rise in [the number of relevant facilities], from [starting figure] in [starting year] to [ending figure] in [ending year]. Together, the materials direct attention to how an expanding public service can become part of everyday life.

A facility makes a practical difference when people can reach it and use it with reasonable effort. A shorter journey makes a brief visit easier to fit into a busy day, while suitable equipment and clear arrangements allow people with different needs to take part. Such benefits depend on more than construction alone. Safety, maintenance and the location of a facility help determine whether it remains welcoming after it opens. The increase in provision is therefore encouraging, but its everyday value is realized through actual use.

Planners should consider where residents live, what obstacles they face and how the facilities will be maintained. Users, for their part, can help preserve the shared environment. By bringing provision and practical needs closer together, a community can turn a larger supply of facilities into a more useful public service.

替换说明：people后用-ing；with后采用“名词＋-ing”结构。the number of后接可数名词复数，谓语由number决定用单数。迁移到图书馆或运动场时，同步更换距离、设备、服务和维护细节。完整应用见2024年范文；表中没有测量的作用机制应作为合理分析表达。

## 论证路线与完整变体

### 阅读与学习：从资源占有走向主动理解

以下保留原讲义完整框架。适用于“拥有资源与真正使用”的对照；2017年可直接观察其完整填充，2022年则需把主线调整为跨领域联系。

The pictures present a contrast between [attitude or behavior A] and [attitude or behavior B]. While [a complete description of the first scene], [a complete description of the second]. This everyday comparison brings into focus a larger question: how can we turn the resources around us into genuine learning?

Access to knowledge is valuable, but understanding requires active engagement. When learners connect a new idea with prior knowledge, examine the reasons behind it and try to explain it in their own words, they move beyond simply receiving information. Consider a student reading about a difficult concept: making notes and working through an example can reveal what remains unclear and point to questions worth exploring. The value of learning therefore lies in what we do with information, as well as in what we acquire.

A practical response is to choose [a relevant resource or activity] with a clear purpose and make time to use it thoughtfully. Regular reading, discussion and application can turn that purpose into a habit. What matters is sustained engagement that deepens understanding, rather than the appearance of being well equipped to learn.

替换说明：between两侧用平行名词短语或-ing，如 *owning books and reading them*；While两侧均为完整句。choose后填名词短语，如 *a book that addresses a question we want to explore*。不能把阅读的解释、提问机制生硬套到所有社会主题。

### 积极主题：解释一种做法如何发挥作用

**路线：具体行动→中间变化→实际收益。** 不必在每一段都罗列“两点原因”。下段适合提前行动、反馈、习惯；2019年坚持题则应换成恢复体力、查找障碍、调整方法。

Starting early is useful because it creates room for feedback and revision. For example, a student preparing a report can show an outline to a classmate, discover which part of the explanation is unclear and revise it before the deadline. The advantage comes from having time to respond to a problem, rather than from simply spending more hours on the task. A small first step can therefore make later work more purposeful.

### 争议主题：承认合理顾虑，再提出判断标准

**路线：承认一方合理需要→指出单一标准的不足→提出兼顾条件。** 适合选课、科技、文化与兴趣，不需要强行得出“凡事都有两面性”。

Convenience is a reasonable consideration, but it should not be the only measure of a worthwhile course. An easy assessment may reduce immediate pressure without giving students much opportunity to develop their abilities. A demanding course, however, is not automatically a better one: its content, support and workload still need to suit the learner’s circumstances. A considered choice therefore weighs both the learning it makes possible and the effort that meaningful participation requires.

### 社会议题：把资源供给连接到实际受益

**路线：供给或机会→使用障碍→必要配套→真实收益。** 适合公共设施、教育资源与科技服务。每次迁移都应更换具体障碍，不能仅替换park为technology。

A nearby park can make a practical difference to daily life. When residents do not have to spend much time or money reaching an outdoor space, a short walk becomes easier to fit into a busy day. Safe paths and regular maintenance also make the space more convenient to use. The value of public facilities therefore lies in how well they meet everyday needs, as well as in how many have been built.

### 图画描述：动作、互动与关键细节

**核心必背**

**The picture shows four people sitting around a dining table, each looking at a mobile phone instead of talking with the others.** *(01)*

_用法：写清共同场景与各人的动作。shows后为“宾语＋-ing”，each looking为补充说明；人物和活动可整体替换。_

**What catches the eye is the gap between the advice being given and the example being set.** *(07)*

_用法：突出言行差距。What从句作主语，主句用is；两处being＋过去分词平行。_

**The water offered by one climber gives the exchange a practical dimension: encouragement is accompanied by help.** *(11)*

_用法：把道具写成互动的一部分。offered by为后置定语；冒号后解释细节怎样服务主旨。_

**优秀变体**

- In the cartoon, two hikers pause on a mountain path, one visibly exhausted and the other offering encouragement. *(02)*

  _用法：以“名词＋补充成分”写两人状态；one后用形容词，the other后用-ing，均承接two hikers，不是两个独立完整句。_

- With his eyes fixed on the screen, the young man seems unaware of the conversation around him. *(03)*

  _用法：视线细节引出注意力状态。with＋宾语＋过去分词，eyes与fix之间是被动关系；seems保留观察语气。_

- A notice on the board gives the two students something to discuss, but they differ over whether the event is worth attending. *(04)*

  _用法：用共同对象连接人物互动。differ over后接whether从句，适合画面中确有意见分歧。_

- The caption turns an ordinary scene into a comment on the way people make everyday choices. *(05)*

  _用法：解释文字提示如何帮助理解画意。comment on后说明具体行为，不能只写空泛social problems。_

- The shelves are full, yet the books appear to function more as possessions than as resources for learning. *(06)*

  _用法：从可见数量转向象征意义。appear与more as…than as…表示解释，前后名词平行。_

- The people share the same physical space without taking part in the same conversation. *(08)*

  _用法：概括“同处而不交流”。without后用-ing，适合手机聚会等明确场景。_

- The child’s enthusiasm is apparent in his costume and his willingness to continue practicing. *(09)*

  _用法：用具体外观和言语支撑情绪判断。be apparent in后说明可见依据，不凭空判断人物性格。_

- Rather than depicting an extraordinary achievement, the drawing focuses on a small decision made in ordinary life. *(10)*

  _用法：说明材料以小见大。rather than后用-ing，与focuses的主语保持一致。_

- The scene leaves the outcome open, directing our attention to the choice the characters face. *(12)*

  _用法：图画没有交代结果时，关注选择而不编造结局。directing的逻辑主语是the scene。_

### 对照与时间：区分行为差异和角色变化

**核心必背**

**On the left, the father watches television while telling his son to study; on the right, he reads beside the child.** *(13)*

_用法：左右分述后再评论。此处while telling的逻辑主语是father，与主句主语一致；先写父亲看电视，避免动作误指儿子。_

**What changes is the person providing practical help, while the bond between them continues.** *(17)*

_用法：用What…while…同时写变化与延续。不能把while一律理解成价值对立。_

**The difference lies less in the resources available than in the willingness to make use of them.** *(21)*

_用法：比较两种解释的相对重要性。less in…than in…后的结构保持平行，并非否认资源作用。_

**优秀变体**

- The contrast is between giving an instruction and providing an example. *(14)*

  _用法：用平行-ing概括真正差异，适合言行示范，不必把差异扩大成好人和坏人。_

- The two pictures offer different answers to the same practical question: when should work begin? *(15)*

  _用法：以共同问题组织两图，适合提前行动与拖延的对照。_

- In the earlier scene, the mother supports the child; years later, the direction of care is reversed. *(16)*

  _用法：时间变化与照护角色相连。be reversed表示方向反转，不表示亲情减弱。_

- The same difficulty produces different responses, depending on what each person chooses to notice. *(18)*

  _用法：适合面对同一损失的不同反应；depending on后接what从句，说明差异来自关注角度。_

- One student sees only an unrelated subject, whereas the other sees a possible source of useful ideas. *(19)*

  _用法：对照对材料价值的判断。whereas连接完整句，不等于两名学生有不同专业。_

- The later picture does more than repeat the earlier gesture: it gives the daughter the role once held by her mother. *(20)*

  _用法：解释跨时间呼应。once held by为过去分词短语，修饰role。_

- Seen together, the scenes suggest continuity within change rather than a simple opposition between past and present. *(22)*

  _用法：概括“变化中的延续”。Seen together的逻辑主语为the scenes，用于关系延续类材料。_

### 动态图表：指标、增量与走势

> [!WARNING]
> **数据单位不能混用。** 每百户台数是平均设备数量，不是拥有该设备的家庭百分比，数值可以超过100。
>
> 从20%到30%是增加10个百分点，相对增长率是50%；不能把10个百分点写成增长10%。

**核心必背**

**The table gives year-end figures for three types of household appliances, measured in units per 100 households.** *(23)*

_用法：先交代统计对象与单位。measured in修饰figures；每百户台数不能写成家庭占比。_

**The number of air conditioners rose from 75.2 to 145.9 units per 100 households over the period shown.** *(24)*

_用法：起止值与单位写完整。rose from A to B；此处保留现有2025年题面记录。_

**All three categories recorded increases, although the scale of the rise varied considerably.** *(25)*

_用法：先总后分。仅在全部类别上升且差异明显时使用。_

**优秀变体**

- Air conditioners showed the largest absolute increase, gaining 70.7 units per 100 households. *(26)*

  _用法：比较绝对增量。gaining后的数值为终值减初值，不是增长率。_

- Washing machines increased more modestly, from 83.7 to 98.2 units per 100 households. *(27)*

  _用法：描述相对较小的增长。more modestly需要有可比较的其他系列。_

- By the end of the period, air conditioners had overtaken the other two categories in average ownership. *(28)*

  _用法：终点排序与先前对比。overtake适合确有排序变化，不能用于只有一个时点的数据。_

- The gap between the two categories widened from [number] to [number] percentage points. *(29)*

  _用法：描述占比差距扩大。两个槽均填“占比之差”，单位是百分点；完整示例：The gap between the two categories widened from 5 to 12 percentage points. 该例为句型填充，不是真题数据。_

- Growth was concentrated in the later part of the period, with relatively little change at the beginning. *(30)*

  _用法：说明分阶段走势。需有中间年份支持，只有起止两值时不使用。_

### 2010年：文化交流与理解

材料：文化“火锅”图。学习：由文化元素并存进入接触、比较和理解，不将融合等同于抹去个性。

The drawing shows a hotpot filled with labels associated with Chinese and foreign cultures. Its caption calls this cultural “hotpot” both delicious and nourishing, suggesting that contact between cultures can be enjoyable and instructive.

Such exchange need not erase the distinctiveness of any tradition. Encountering an unfamiliar way of thinking can prompt people to examine familiar assumptions and appreciate forms of expression they have not met before. For example, reading literature from another culture may reveal a different account of family life while also showing themes shared with works at home. Comparing these perspectives can broaden understanding, provided readers approach them with curiosity, sufficient context and a willingness to reflect.

We should remain open to learning across cultural boundaries while developing a thoughtful understanding of our own traditions. Schools and cultural institutions can support this process through accessible translations, performances and discussion. Individuals can take part by reading, listening and asking informed questions. A cultural “hotpot” is most nourishing when people seek to understand the traditions behind each element.

### 2011年：环境责任与公共空间

材料：水上游览与遗留垃圾。学习：个人省事如何转化为公共环境和他人的清理负担。

The drawing shows tourists enjoying a boat trip while rubbish floats on the water around them. Bottles, food scraps and other waste remain after the visitors have passed. The caption draws attention to what is left behind, creating an ironic contrast between the pleasure of a journey and the damage caused by careless disposal.

The problem is that a moment of personal convenience can create a burden for a shared place. Throwing away packaging may save one visitor the trouble of carrying it, but the waste does not disappear. It makes the surroundings less pleasant and leaves other people to collect what has been discarded. When visitors treat a scenic area as a space for enjoyment without considering its upkeep, they undermine the conditions that made the visit worthwhile. Respect for the environment therefore needs to appear in ordinary actions, not only in statements about its importance.

Tourists should keep their rubbish until they find a suitable collection point and avoid leaving food or packaging in the water. Tour operators can support this behavior by providing bins where practical, arranging collection and giving clear instructions before a trip. Local managers should also respond to repeated dumping. These measures connect responsibility with the actual handling of waste, helping ensure that one person's journey does not become another person's cleanup task.

### 2012年：积极心态与具体行动

材料：面对打翻瓶子与剩余液体的不同反应。学习：承认损失，再从剩余资源找到具体行动。

The drawing shows a fallen bottle with some liquid left inside. Two people react to the same situation in opposite ways: one says that the drink is gone, while the other points out that some liquid remains. Their different responses suggest that how we interpret a setback can influence what we do next.

A constructive attitude does not require us to pretend that no loss has occurred. It helps us distinguish the damage from the resources still available. Someone who sees only failure may stop looking for a workable next step. By contrast, a person who notices what remains has a starting point for action. A student disappointed by an assignment, for example, can examine a particular weakness and use later practice to address it. The result of one attempt need not become a final judgment on every future effort. What matters is turning a realistic assessment into a response that is both specific and possible.

We should therefore acknowledge difficulties without allowing them to define the whole situation. Asking what can still be used, what needs to change and whom we might consult can make optimism practical. Such questions do not guarantee success, but they help preserve the willingness to act. The value of a positive outlook lies in that connection between recognizing possibilities and doing something with them.

### 2013年：毕业选择与自我评估

材料：毕业生面临不同去向。学习：将路径要求与个人能力、资源和目标进行比较。

The drawing places a group of graduates before four paths: finding a job, pursuing postgraduate study, going abroad and starting a business. The caption, “Choice,” draws attention to the decision they face. It suggests that graduation is not simply an ending, but a point at which people must consider how to direct their next stage of effort.

A useful decision begins with understanding both the options and oneself. The same path may serve different purposes for different people. Further study can be valuable when a field requires deeper training, while work may offer experience needed to test a particular interest. Neither label guarantees a satisfying result. Graduates need to examine requirements, costs and the kind of activity involved, then compare these with their abilities, resources and aims. Advice can help them discover information they have overlooked, but choosing only because friends have made the same decision may leave important personal needs unexamined.

Universities can support this process by providing clear information and opportunities to talk with people who have followed different routes. Graduates, for their part, can identify a manageable next step and review their plans as they gain experience. Planning need not eliminate every uncertainty before action begins. Its value lies in giving a decision a reasoned basis and leaving room to learn from what happens next.

### 2014年：家庭照护与尊重

材料：母女在不同生命阶段相互照护。学习：时间变化、角色转换、实际支持与尊严。

The two pictures show a mother and daughter at different stages of life. Thirty years ago, the mother held her young daughter's hand as they walked together. In the present scene, the grown daughter supports her ageing mother. The change in their roles suggests that care can continue within a family even as the person giving practical support changes.

Such care is meaningful because it responds to the needs of a particular person. A child depends on an adult for guidance and safety; an older parent may later need help with activities once managed alone. Recognizing this change requires attention rather than a general declaration of gratitude. Spending time together allows family members to notice difficulties, listen to preferences and decide what assistance would actually be useful. It also helps preserve the older person's dignity. Support should not mean taking every decision away from someone simply because age has brought new limitations.

Adult children can therefore turn affection into regular, practical acts: keeping in touch, making time for visits and discussing arrangements when help is needed. Responsibilities can be shared according to each person's circumstances, with outside services considered where appropriate. The point is not to reproduce one fixed family arrangement. It is to keep concern responsive and respectful as lives change, so that the companionship shown in the pictures remains more than a memory of the past.

### 2015年：面对面交流与注意力

材料：共同聚餐时各自看手机。学习：具体解释注意力如何影响倾听和回应，不全盘否定手机。

The picture shows four people sitting around a dining table, each looking at a mobile phone instead of talking with the others. Although they have gathered in the same place, their attention is directed elsewhere. The caption, “A gathering in the age of mobile phones,” highlights the difference between being physically present and taking part in a shared conversation.

A conversation depends on more than exchanging words. People notice whether others listen, respond and show interest in what has been said. When attention repeatedly shifts to a screen, these small signs of engagement may be lost. Someone who has come to share an experience can feel that speaking is less worthwhile when no one appears ready to listen. This is not an argument against the value of phones. They allow people at a distance to remain in contact and can provide useful information during a meeting. The difficulty arises when their use replaces the interaction that brought the group together.

A practical response is to make room for both connection and consideration. Friends can agree to put their phones aside during a meal, while allowing someone to explain if an urgent message needs attention. Individuals can also pause before checking a notification and ask whether it can wait. Such habits keep the device in a supporting role. They help ensure that the people around the table receive the attention for which the gathering was intended.

### 2016年：以身作则与建议的可信度

材料：父亲言语要求与亲自示范的左右场景。学习：言行一致如何使指导更可信。

The drawings compare two situations involving a father and his son. On the left, the father watches television while ordering the child to study. On the right, he reads at a desk while his son also studies. The caption emphasizes setting an example, suggesting that a parent’s own behavior gives meaning to the advice offered to a child.

This message concerns consistency as much as discipline. Children may find a demand less convincing when the adult making it repeatedly acts in the opposite way. By contrast, a parent who makes time for reading demonstrates that learning deserves attention in everyday life. This visible example makes advice more concrete than a repeated instruction to “work harder.” It presents learning as a shared practice rather than a one-sided demand.

Parents should therefore connect reasonable expectations with their own conduct. This does not mean studying beside children at every moment or measuring good parenting only by examination results. It means giving advice practical credibility, offering support when needed and allowing children to develop responsibility for their own learning. A consistent example makes that guidance easier to understand and trust.

### 2017年：拥有书籍与实际阅读

材料：拥有很多书与设定阅读目标的对照。学习：从资源占有进入主动思考和持续使用。

The drawings contrast owning many books with setting a reading goal. In the first, a man sits in front of a full bookcase and says that he has so many books. In the second, a man reads at a desk and says that he aims to finish twenty books this year. Together, the pictures invite us to consider what turns access to learning materials into actual learning.

Books offer possibilities, but their value is realized through attention and thought. A reader can follow an idea, connect it with prior knowledge and decide what to make of it. Simply adding another title to a collection does not by itself create understanding. The same distinction applies to online courses: registering provides access, while taking notes, testing an explanation and using an idea can help turn access into learning. A modest collection used carefully can therefore be more useful than a large one that remains unread.

The practical response is to link resources with a workable reading routine. We can choose books that serve a clear purpose, set aside regular time and briefly explain what we have learned after each session. A yearly target may help maintain direction, but the number completed should not replace depth of understanding. What matters is making thoughtful use of the books we have, rather than treating possession itself as an achievement.

### 2018年：学习价值与合理负担一起评估

材料：选课系统中的学习价值与便利取向。学习：承认分数、负担的合理性，再形成兼顾标准。

In the cartoon, a student studies the options on a course-selection system. One set of features emphasizes new knowledge, innovation and difficulty, while the other highlights high marks, an easy pass and little homework. The scene asks us to consider whether choosing a course is mainly a search for convenience or a decision about what is worth learning.

Grades and workload matter, but they do not describe the whole value of a course. A class may require effort because it asks students to question an explanation, solve an unfamiliar problem or develop an idea of their own. Working through those tasks can produce abilities that remain useful after the examination is over. Choosing only what is easiest may remove opportunities to discover and improve a weakness. At the same time, difficulty alone does not make a course worthwhile. The content should be relevant, the teaching should support learning and the demands should be possible within a student's wider responsibilities.

Students can therefore look beyond a simple promise of easy marks. Reading course descriptions, checking requirements and asking how the work is assessed can help them make an informed choice. Once enrolled, they should give the learning process enough attention to benefit from it. Universities can assist by making expectations and support clear. The aim is not to choose the hardest option every time, but to seek a reasonable challenge that develops something more lasting than a convenient result.

### 2019年：坚持与调整

材料：登山途中疲惫、同伴递水和建议休息后继续。学习：坚持与恢复、调整方法相互支持。

In the cartoon, two people pause during a climb. One is so tired that he wants to give up, while his companion offers him water and suggests continuing after a short rest. The scene highlights perseverance, but the proposed break also shows that persistence need not mean ignoring fatigue.

Difficult goals demand sustained effort, but not a constant pace. When progress becomes slow, people may mistake temporary exhaustion for proof that the whole task is impossible. A pause can help them recover and judge the next step more clearly. They may also need to change their approach rather than repeat an ineffective method. A learner struggling with a demanding chapter, for instance, could review a missing basic idea before returning to the harder material. Continuing in this way preserves the goal while making the route more workable. Encouragement from another person can be valuable because it draws attention to an option that frustration has hidden.

We should therefore connect perseverance with sensible preparation and adjustment. Dividing a long task into manageable stages, allowing time to recover and seeking useful advice can help sustain effort. None of this makes success automatic, and genuine dangers may call for a different decision. Perseverance therefore means neither quitting at the first setback nor ignoring a genuine risk; it means choosing a manageable next step.

### 2020年：动手时机与工作习惯

材料：提前完成与拖到最后的工作习惯对照。学习：以反馈和应对意外的时间解释提前行动。

The two drawings present different approaches to work. On the left, a student works at a desk and says that finishing early brings peace of mind. On the right, another student leans back in a chair, saying that he will not begin until the last moment. The caption, “Habits,” suggests that the contrast is about repeated choices rather than one unusually busy day.

Starting in good time has a practical advantage: it leaves room for uncertainty. A task that looks simple may require extra reading, access to a needed resource, or help from someone else. For example, a student who prepares an outline early can discover a weak argument, ask for feedback and revise the report before submission. Someone who postpones the same task may have less time to make those adjustments, especially if an unexpected difficulty arises.

Developing a better habit does not require completing everything immediately. Students can break a larger assignment into manageable steps, begin with a small first task such as reading the instructions, and set aside time to review the work. This can make it easier to start while preserving room for later adjustments.

### 2021年：支持真实兴趣，也让兴趣经受实践

材料：孩子学习戏曲的兴趣与同伴态度。学习：尊重兴趣，同时以练习和指导解释能力形成。

In the drawing, a boy dressed for an opera performance tells his father that many classmates do not find learning opera enjoyable. His father responds that the boy's own interest is enough reason to continue. The conversation suggests that a genuine interest deserves consideration even when it is not shared by the people around us.

Interest can make the effort of learning feel worthwhile. Someone who enjoys an activity is more willing to spend time on its details, return after a disappointing attempt and notice gradual improvement. In learning opera, for example, practicing a difficult line or movement can become meaningful because it helps the learner perform something he values. This does not mean enthusiasm alone produces skill. Practice and useful guidance still matter. Nor does following an interest require rejecting every suggestion from others. The important distinction is between advice that helps us learn and pressure to abandon an activity merely because it is unfashionable among our peers.

Parents and teachers can therefore support exploration without deciding too quickly what a child ought to enjoy. They can listen, provide a suitable opportunity to practice and help the learner judge the experience honestly. Children, in turn, can give an interest sustained attention before making their own choices. The father's encouragement is valuable because it gives the boy room to discover what matters to him through action.

### 2022年：专业之外的讲座

材料：是否参加专业之外讲座的分歧。学习：通过跨领域方法的实际运用说明学习价值。

The picture shows two students discussing a campus lecture advertised on a noticeboard. One dismisses it because it is not related to her major, while the other believes that attending could be useful. Their disagreement raises a question about whether the value of learning should be judged only by the label of a subject.

Specialist study is important, but a different field may offer a way of thinking that makes familiar questions easier to understand. A science student listening to a lecture on communication, for example, might learn how to explain a technical idea to an audience without the same background. A lecture on history may also reveal how a problem developed, helping students see assumptions they had previously taken for granted. The benefit comes from connecting and examining ideas, rather than simply collecting more information. Dismissing a lecture solely because it falls outside one’s major may prevent students from discovering useful connections.

Students should therefore approach unfamiliar subjects with curiosity and judgment. They need not attend every available lecture or neglect the work required in their own field. They can read an outline, identify a question they want to explore and decide whether an event is worth their time. Afterward, discussing or applying one useful idea can turn brief exposure into learning. An open mind is most valuable when it leads to thoughtful engagement.

### 2023年：传统参与与代际传递

材料：村庄龙舟赛与观看者的评价。学习：参与、学习和合作如何延续传统。

The drawing shows a dragon-boat race on a river, with spectators gathered on a bridge. Two villagers watch the event, and one happily remarks that their village's race is becoming livelier. The scene suggests that a tradition remains meaningful when people continue to take part in it.

Shared activities let residents learn cultural practices through participation, not only from written descriptions. Participants must learn how an event works, practice relevant skills and cooperate with others. Meanwhile, spectators can become curious about its history and the customs surrounding it. A village race thus offers more than a moment of entertainment: it gives residents a common experience through which a local tradition can be understood and renewed. Its value does not depend solely on attracting an ever larger crowd, but also on the connections that participation creates.

To sustain that value, organizers can make events welcoming to different generations. Experienced participants can explain the customs, while organizers can offer newcomers suitable ways to help or learn. Safety and reasonable arrangements also matter, since a celebration should be enjoyable for both participants and spectators. Preserving tradition therefore need not mean leaving it untouched in the past. By creating opportunities for people to participate responsibly, a community can keep inherited practices part of its present life.

### 2024年：公共设施与日常生活

材料：居民使用公园的图画与城市公园数量变化。学习：两种材料分别交代，再论证可达性和维护。

The picture shows residents exercising in a park, with one person praising the new park near home. The accompanying chart records an increase in the number of parks in a particular city, from 406 in 2020 to 532 in 2021 and 670 in 2022. Together, they suggest that a larger park supply matters most when residents can actually reach and use the parks.

A nearby park can make a practical difference. When residents do not have to spend much time or money reaching an outdoor space, a short walk or exercise session becomes easier to fit into a busy day. Older people can also benefit from places where they can rest, meet neighbors and remain active without traveling far. For families, a shared space offers opportunities to spend time together outside the home. These benefits depend on more than construction alone: safe paths, suitable equipment and regular maintenance help turn a completed park into a place that people can actually use.

The increase in park provision is therefore encouraging, but the next step is to make those spaces welcoming and useful. Local planners should consider where residents live and what different age groups need, while users should take care of the shared environment. A well-used neighborhood park can become a lasting part of community life.

### 2025年：每百户家电拥有量

材料：全国每百户家电拥有量的跨年统计。学习：先比总体与增量，再解释需求、可能条件和产品的实际用途。

The table gives year-end figures for three types of household appliances, measured in units per 100 households nationwide, from 2014 to 2023. Air conditioners recorded the largest increase, rising from 75.2 to 145.9 units per 100 households. Over the same period, washing machines increased from 83.7 to 98.2, while refrigerators and freezers rose from 85.5 to 103.4. The figures increased across all three categories, though at different rates.

Greater affordability, easier access to products and changing household needs may help explain this pattern. A reliable washing machine can reduce time spent on routine laundry, while a refrigerator makes it easier to keep food for later use. Air conditioners can make indoor spaces more comfortable in hot weather, and some households may need more than one unit for different rooms. These practical uses give families reasons to acquire appliances when their resources permit. The averages do not reveal how ownership is distributed, but they invite us to consider how useful products can meet everyday needs.

These increases matter only if appliances meet genuine needs and remain reliable and efficient. Manufacturers should therefore prioritize product quality, energy efficiency and repair access rather than simply encouraging households to buy more. Such measures could help households benefit from ownership growth while limiting unnecessary costs and waste.

### 2026年：科技服务与使用条件

材料：养老机器人接受程度与主要顾虑的两组统计。学习：分别描述分布，以可靠性、成本和服务回应使用条件。

The charts report a survey of consumer attitudes toward eldercare robots and their primary concerns. Full acceptance stands at 39.3%, while 32.8% express partial acceptance and 27.9% do not accept the technology. Among the concerns shown, safety ranks first at 46.3%, followed by price at 24.9% and convenience at 10.7%. Together, the charts suggest that interest in the technology coexists with substantial reservations.

This combination is understandable. Families may welcome equipment that helps with routine care, especially when relatives cannot always be present. Yet usefulness depends on reliable performance. A device that moves unpredictably or gives confusing instructions may create additional work and anxiety instead of offering support. Safety therefore deserves attention throughout design, testing and everyday use. Cost matters as well: even a helpful robot will have limited value to a family that cannot afford its purchase or maintenance. Clear controls and accessible assistance can make the technology easier to use with confidence.

The aim should be to provide dependable support for older people, rather than to introduce machines for their own sake. Manufacturers should demonstrate reliability, explain limitations clearly and offer responsive maintenance. Families and care providers can then decide where robots are useful while preserving the personal contact that good care also requires.
