### 邀请参加活动

*适用任务：邀请对方出席活动，交代参与方式与必要安排。*

Dear [Name],

I would like to invite you to [活动名词短语], which will be held [时间和地点状语]. The event will bring together [参与者名词短语] to [目的：动词原形], and we would be delighted if you could join us.

The program will include [主要活动名词短语], with time set aside for [交流环节名词或动名词短语]. We hope you will enjoy the occasion and the chance to [具体参与方式：动词原形]. If you need any assistance with the arrangements, I would be happy to help.

Please let me know whether you will be able to attend. Thank you for considering our invitation.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**替换示例：** invite you to our cultural exhibition；to exchange ideas about traditional crafts；with time set aside for questions and discussion。`invite somebody to`后接活动，`invite somebody to do`后接动作。典礼突出流程和出席协助，交流活动突出参与机会。完整应用见2018年范文。

### 邀请担任评委

*适用任务：邀请担任评委，明确评价任务与反馈职责。*

Dear [Name],

I am writing to invite you to serve as a judge for [比赛名称], which will take place [时间和地点状语].

Each participant will [比赛任务：动词原形短语]. We would appreciate your assessment of [评价维度名词短语], followed by a few comments to help the participants improve. To make preparation easier, we will send you the program and judging criteria in advance.

Please let me know whether you would be available. We would be delighted to have your support and will gladly provide any further information you need.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**用途：** 受邀职责是评价与反馈，不能只写活动多么有趣。`assessment of`可填the speakers’ ideas, organization and delivery；`followed by`说明评价之后的反馈。完整应用见2013年范文。

### 邀请组织参赛团队

*适用任务：邀请教授组织参赛团队，而非邀请其本人参赛。*

Dear Professor [Surname],

I am writing to invite you to organize a team for [比赛名称], which will be held at [主办学校名称].

The contest offers students an opportunity to [参赛价值：动词原形短语]. Your guidance could help bring together students with complementary skills and turn a promising idea into a workable project. If you are willing to take on this role, I would be happy to share the entry requirements and discuss how the team could prepare.

Thank you for considering our invitation. Please let me know whether you would be interested in organizing a team.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

> [!WARNING]
> **不要改变受邀角色。** 请求对方组织队伍，主体写团队组成与筹备支持；不能误改成邀请教授本人来参赛。`complementary skills`指相互补充的技能；`take on this role`指承担职责。完整应用见2022年范文。

### 给朋友提出准备建议

*适用任务：为朋友提出按步骤落实的准备建议。*

Dear [Name],

I am glad to hear that you are planning to [任务：动词原形短语]. Here are a few suggestions that may help you prepare.

First, [具体行动：祈使句], so that [直接作用：完整分句]. Once you have [准备动作：过去分词短语], you could [下一行动：动词原形短语], paying particular attention to [关键细节名词短语]. Before [关键环节：动名词短语], it would be useful to [检查或练习：动词原形短语]. This would give you a chance to identify any difficulties and make the necessary adjustments.

If you would like, I would be happy to [具体帮助：动词原形短语]. I hope these suggestions will be useful, and I wish you every success with [任务名词短语].

Yours,  
Li Ming

**填充示例：** First, choose one scientist and focus on a particular achievement, so that your report has a clear central idea. Once you have checked the main facts, you could organize the material around the problem, the contribution and its significance, paying particular attention to what your audience needs to understand.

`Once you have`后接过去分词，组成现在完成时；`paying…`的逻辑主语是you。求职应按岗位要求、申请材料与面试组织，报告应按选题、查证、组织与排练组织，不机械共用行动内容。见2021、2024年范文。

### 向学校或机构提出改进建议

*适用任务：向有实施权限的机构提出服务或设施改进建议。*

Dear [Title or Name],

I am writing to suggest ways [机构名称] could make [服务或活动名词短语] more accessible to [使用者名词短语].

One useful step would be to [改进措施：动词原形短语], with a clear timetable to help users plan their visits. It would also be worth offering [支持方式名词短语] for those who are unfamiliar with the service. By taking different needs into account, the institution could make it easier for more people to participate and benefit from what is available.

Thank you for considering these suggestions. I would be glad to help gather users’ views on the proposed changes.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**变换条件：** 这篇适合设施、开放时间和参与支持。若问题与到访无关，应同时替换`timetable / visits / users`。`It would be worth offering`中的worth后接动名词。见2014年范文。

### 推荐书籍或电影

*适用任务：为阅读、观影或共同讨论推荐合适作品。*

Dear [Name],

I would like to recommend [作品名称] for [your reading / our next discussion]. Its treatment of [作品实际涉及的问题名词短语] makes it a good choice for this occasion.

What I find especially engaging is [作品具体特点名词短语]. Rather than simply presenting an answer, it invites us to consider [值得讨论的问题：名词短语或疑问词从句]. We could focus our discussion on one scene or passage and compare the different ways of understanding it. This would give everyone a clear starting point while leaving room for personal responses.

I hope you will give it a try. I look forward to hearing what you think.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**用法：** 这篇适合具有讨论价值的作品；不适合原封不动推荐工具书或纯娱乐节目。`What…is…`后可填the way the story presents friendship；书写passage，电影写scene。2011、2015年范文分别展示电影推荐与读书会荐书。

### 推荐两个不同的去处

*适用任务：推荐两处特点不同的地点，并分别说明理由。*

Dear [Name],

Welcome to [城市名称]. I would like to recommend two places that would give you different ways to experience the city.

The first is [地点一名称], where you can [与该地点相符的活动：动词原形短语]. Its [具体特色名词短语] would be particularly interesting if you enjoy [兴趣：名词或动名词短语]. For a different experience, you could visit [地点二名称]. There you can [另一活动：动词原形短语] and discover [另一特色名词短语]. A visit to both would combine [体验一名词短语] with [体验二名词短语].

If either place interests you, I would be happy to help you plan your visit. I hope you enjoy your stay.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**用途：** 两处分别给理由，形成“历史与日常生活”“室内参观与户外体验”等真实区别。`where`修饰地点；第二处不能只将第一处的形容词换一遍。完整填充见2017年范文。

### 回复咨询并说明参与办法

*适用任务：直接回答项目咨询，说明职责、要求与参与办法。*

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your interest in [项目或活动名称]. Here is the information you requested.

The project involves [主要工作：名词或动名词短语], and volunteers will help [具体职责：动词原形短语]. Before deciding whether to join, please consider [必要时间投入或参与要求名词短语]. We will provide [适当支持名词短语], so that participants know what to expect and how to prepare.

If you would like to take part, please let me know [需要告知的信息：名词短语或疑问词从句]. I would be happy to answer any further questions.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**用法：** `involves`后接名词或动名词，如leading reading activities；`let me know`后可填which activities interest you。来信仅询问信息时，重点是回答问题，不能强加协助职责。见2019年范文。

### 回复帮助意愿并明确分工

*适用任务：承接帮助意愿，说明展示内容并落实双方分工。*

Dear [Name],

Thank you for offering to help with [活动名称]. Here is what we have planned.

We will present [展示内容名词短语], showing how [制作或设计过程：完整分句]. Visitors will be able to [参与方式：动词原形短语], so we need to prepare both the display and the materials beforehand. Could you help us arrange the display tables and prepare the instruction cards while I check the materials for the demonstration? Clear instructions will make it easier for visitors to follow the process.

Please let me know whether this arrangement works for you. I appreciate your willingness to help.

Yours,  
Li Ming

**变换条件：** 展示内容与分工应同时替换。`while`后为完整分句，分工句可改为Could you help us check the equipment while I prepare the slides? 完整应用见2025年范文。

### 活动通知

*适用任务：向目标读者公布活动内容、参与方式和报名安排。*

Notice

[主办方名称] will hold [活动名称] at [地点] on [日期]. Students interested in [活动主题：名词或动名词短语] are welcome to take part.

The event will begin with [开场环节名词短语], followed by [主要环节名词短语]. Participants will have an opportunity to [具体体验：动词原形短语]. Those who wish to take part should register at [报名地点] by [截止日期] and provide [必要信息名词短语]. Further details about the program will be available from [咨询部门或渠道].

Please arrive before the event begins. We look forward to welcoming everyone interested.

[题目要求的署名]

**用法：** 通知不用Dear或Yours。报名手续仅在需要时加入；`by`表示截止时间。`begin with…followed by…`写顺序，不能替代活动内容。完整示例见2020年范文。

### 设施与服务说明通知

*适用任务：说明设施怎样使用、办理什么手续、遇到问题如何求助。*

Notice

Welcome to [设施名称]. This notice provides the information you need to make use of its services.

The facility is open [开放时间状语]. To use [服务名称], bring [所需材料名词短语] to [服务地点], where staff can explain the arrangements and answer questions. You can also find [资源名词短语] through [可用系统名称]. Please follow the instructions provided and return any borrowed items on time so that others can use them.

If you need help finding materials or using the system, ask at the help desk. Staff will be happy to assist you.

[题目要求的署名]

**用法：** 先写读者如何使用，再写规则与求助。借还要求适用于图书馆等借用服务；换成体育设施或咨询服务时，应相应替换。见2016年范文。

### 招募通知

*适用任务：招募人员，写清职责、资格与申请渠道。*

Notice

One student assistant is needed for [项目名称]. The selected applicant will help [主要工作：动词原形短语] and [相关任务：动词原形短语].

Applicants should be able to [职责所需能力：动词原形短语] and work carefully with [工作对象或材料名词短语]. Reliability and a willingness to communicate are important because the work involves [具体协作：名词或动名词短语]. Relevant experience is welcome where it helps with these responsibilities.

Interested students may contact [负责人或部门名称] to learn more about the work and how to apply. Please include a brief description of your relevant skills when submitting an application.

[题目要求的署名]

**人数与谓语：** 单岗位用One student assistant is needed；多人用Volunteers are needed。岗位职责决定资格：问卷研究需要沟通、数据整理和细致；接待会议需要英语交流与会务协助。`Relevant experience is preferred but not essential.`仅在经验并非硬性条件时使用。见2010、2023年范文。

### 请求专业帮助

*适用任务：请求范围明确、方便对方回应的专业帮助。*

Dear [Name],

I am preparing [报告、项目或材料名称] and would appreciate your advice on [需要指导的问题名词短语]. Your experience in [相关领域名词短语] would be especially helpful at this stage.

I have already [已完成准备：过去分词短语], but I am still unsure how to [具体困难：动词原形短语]. Would you be willing to look at [具体材料名词短语] and suggest how I could improve it? I can send you a short outline in advance, so that you will not need to work through the whole draft. If a meeting is possible, I would be glad to fit in with your schedule.

Thank you for considering my request. Please let me know whether this would be convenient for you.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**填充示例：** I have already collected the main sources, but I am still unsure how to organize the argument. 请求要具体，也应尽量降低对方的时间成本；不在正文写成无边界的“请替我完成”。本篇为补充交际训练。

### 感谢具体帮助

*适用任务：感谢具体帮助，并说明实际受益。*

Dear [Name],

Thank you for the time you spent going through my draft. Your comments helped me see which parts needed a clearer explanation.

I especially appreciated the way you asked questions from a reader’s point of view. After revising the structure and adding an example, I was able to explain the central idea more clearly. I have also learned to check whether each paragraph gives the reader enough information.

Thank you again for your thoughtful help. I hope I will have an opportunity to support your work in return.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**迁移：** 把“修改文章”整体换成“活动准备”“适应校园”等真实帮助；保留具体帮助、实际受益、再次致谢的连贯关系。本篇为完整填充示例，不标作独立真题。

### 道歉并提出可行替代

*适用任务：说明无法履约的情况，致歉并提出可行替代。*

Dear [Name],

I am sorry that I cannot meet you as planned because my schedule has changed unexpectedly. I understand that this may cause inconvenience.

Would the first Saturday of next month be convenient for another meeting? If that does not suit you, please suggest a time that works better. In the meantime, I can send you the materials we intended to discuss so that you will not have to wait for the information.

Thank you for your understanding. I hope we can find a convenient alternative.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**迁移：** 保留必要理由与补救；替代日期必须与原情境相容。`would…be convenient`询问方便与否，不预先替对方作决定。本篇为补充训练。

### 申请与自荐

*适用任务：申请岗位，用相关经历证明能够承担职责。*

Dear [Name or Title],

I am writing to apply for [岗位名称]. The opportunity to [岗位主要任务：动词原形短语] interests me, and I would like to contribute my relevant skills.

During [相关经历名词短语], I [实际行动：过去时谓语短语], which helped me develop [相关技能名词短语]. I am also able to [另一能力：动词原形短语] and can make time for [所需时间投入名词短语]. These preparations would help me carry out the duties described in your notice.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**填充示例：** During a class research project, I organized questionnaire responses, which helped me develop the care needed for accurate data entry. 经历与岗位有关即可；不要把未给定的学历、奖项当成必须编造的装饰。情境允许的自拟经历应前后一致。本篇为补充训练。

### 投诉并请求解决

*适用任务：陈述可观察的问题与实际影响，提出合理解决请求。*

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing about [产品或服务名称] that I purchased from you on [日期]. Unfortunately, [可观察的问题：名词短语或名词性从句] has prevented me from using it as intended.

The problem occurs when [发生条件：完整分句], and it has affected [具体使用名词短语]. Could you please arrange [适当补救名词短语]? If a replacement is available, please let me know how to return the faulty item and what information you need from me.

I would appreciate your help in resolving this matter. Thank you for your attention.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

**替换要求：** `[可观察的问题]`填名词短语或名词性从句，例如a fault in the charging port，或the fact that it will not charge；不能填一个独立句后直接续接has。完整填充可写：Unfortunately, a fault in the charging port has prevented me from using it as intended. 服务投诉应删除不相干的换货信息。本篇为通用交际补充。

### 会议纪要补充示例

*适用任务：记录会议讨论、决定与后续责任分工。*

Minutes of the Planning Meeting

The meeting was held on [日期] at [地点] to discuss arrangements for [活动名称]. Members reviewed the registration procedure and agreed to use one shared form.

[姓名] will prepare the form by [截止日期], while [另一姓名] will check the information required from participants. The coordinator will circulate the final version after these checks. Members also discussed [未解决问题名词短语] and agreed to collect further information before making a decision.

The meeting ended after the responsibilities and next steps had been confirmed.

Prepared by [name]

**用法：** 记录讨论与决定用过去时；写清后续责任人与期限。它是通用功能补充，不将其列为2010—2026年英语（一）独立考题。

### 邀请与角色说明

**核心必背**

**We would be delighted if you could join us for the opening of our cultural exhibition next Friday.** *(01)*

_用法：诚恳邀请出席；join us for后接活动，适合正式而友好的语气。_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **核心必背 · 明确受邀角色**
>
> **I am writing to invite you to serve as a judge for our English speech contest and offer the speakers some feedback.** *(02)*
>
> _用法：邀请评委，同时明确反馈职责；serve as后接角色。_

**Your guidance could help bring together students with complementary skills and develop their ideas into a workable project.** *(03)*

_用法：邀请组织团队时说明对方的实际作用；不能无条件套用到普通出席邀请。_

**优秀变体**

- Given our shared interest in traditional culture, I thought you might enjoy the exhibition. *(04)*

  _用法：用共同兴趣说明私人邀请的理由；shared interest须与情境相符。_

- We would be grateful if you could give a short talk on how your research connects with everyday life. *(05)*

  _用法：邀请作专题发言，明确内容和深度；give a short talk on后接主题。_

- The discussion would benefit from your experience of working with international teams. *(06)*

  _用法：以相关经验说明为何邀请此人；experience of后接动名词。_

- Would you be willing to lead a small group discussion after the presentations? *(07)*

  _用法：请求承担活动中的具体环节；比仅邀请参加多了一项主持职责。_

Dear Paul,

Here are a few suggestions for preparing your report on an ancient Chinese scientist.

First, choose one scientist and focus on a particular achievement. Use reliable library or museum sources to check the main facts. Then organize your report around three questions: What problem did the scientist address? What was the contribution? Why did it matter? Finally, rehearse aloud and ask a friend whether any part is unclear.

If you would like, we could go over your outline together. I hope your presentation goes well.

Yours,  
Li Ming
